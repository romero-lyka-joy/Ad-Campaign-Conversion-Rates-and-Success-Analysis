## 📝 About this Project
In this project, I analyzed a synthetic marketing dataset from Kaggle to gather insights on what contributes to campaign success of the marketing team.

## 📁 Files in this Repository

### 📊 Tableau Data Visualization
I used combination of Figma and Tableau in creating the overall success dashboard of the campaigns. I integrated the ff:
* Overall Success rate and budget
* Success Rate per platform
* Top 10 Most Clicked Campaign
* Cost Per Click vs Conversion Rates
* Demographics Wise success rates
For these data viz, I focused mainly on the overall success to serve as an easy reference for stakeholders to understand how the campaigns are doing in general. I will be focusing on in-depth analysis based on conversion rates and demoghraphics on the python analysis below. This data viz gives us an overview that the campaign is an overall success.
> [Click here for the live dashboard](https://public.tableau.com/app/profile/romerolykajoy/viz/AdvertisingCampaignDashboard_17388379989660/AdvertisingCampaignDashboard)

### 📉 Python Data Analysis and Data Visualization
I inspected and cleaned the data through kaggle (where I also found the dataset). I then made descriptive analyses and data visualization to understand the campaign better. All of my conclusions and recommendations are contained in the same notebook.
